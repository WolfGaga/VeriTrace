For research collaborations, evaluation studies, industry applications and technical partnerships, contact **[hz496@cam.ac.uk](mailto:hz496@cam.ac.uk?subject=VeriTrace%20collaboration)**. Briefly describe your organization, use case and the kind of collaboration you have in mind.
